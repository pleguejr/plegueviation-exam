# Cessna 172N Skyhawk Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH) Expert

This skill provides exact, verified performance data, limitations, and operational procedures for the **Cessna 172N Skyhawk** (Lycoming O-320-H2AD engine, 160 BHP @ 2700 RPM).

## 2. Normal Operating Speeds (POH Section 4)

- **Rotation Speed ($V_R$)**: **55 KIAS** (Flaps UP).
- **Best Angle of Climb ($V_X$)**: **59 KIAS** (Sea Level) to **61 KIAS** (10,000 ft).
- **Best Rate of Climb ($V_Y$)**: **73 KIAS** (Sea Level) to **68 KIAS** (10,000 ft).
- **Enroute Climb (Cruise Climb)**: **75 – 85 KIAS** (Flaps UP).
- **Final Approach ($V_{REF}$)**:
  * Flaps UP: **65 – 75 KIAS**.
  * Flaps 40°: **60 – 70 KIAS**.
  * Short Field (Flaps 40° over 50 ft): **61 KIAS**.
- **Balked Landing / Go-Around**: **55 KIAS** initial climb with Flaps 20°.
- **Maximum Demonstrated Crosswind**: **15 knots**.

## 3. Emergency Procedures & Speeds (POH Section 3)

- **Best Glide Speed ($V_{GLIDE}$)**: **65 KIAS** (Flaps UP, 2300 lbs).
  * Glide distance: ~1.5 NM per 1,000 ft AGL.
- **Engine Failure Immediately After Takeoff**: **65 KIAS** (Flaps UP) / **60 KIAS** (Flaps DOWN).
- **Forced Landing Without Engine Power**:
  * Approach: **65 KIAS** (Flaps UP).
  * Final over obstacle: **60 KIAS** (Flaps 40°).
- **Precautionary Landing With Engine Power**:
  * Inspection: **60 KIAS** (Flaps 20°).
  * Final: **55 KIAS** (Flaps 40°).
- **Ditching**: **55 – 60 KIAS** with Flaps 20°–40° into wind or parallel to swell.

## 4. Powerplant & Fuel Specifications

- **Engine**: Lycoming O-320-H2AD (4 cylinders, horizontally opposed, carbureted, 160 BHP @ 2700 RPM).
- **Fuel Grade**: 100LL (Blue) or 100 (Green).
- **Fuel Capacity**:
  * Standard Tanks: 43 US gal total (39 US gal usable).
  * Long Range Tanks: 54 US gal total (50 US gal usable).
- **Oil Capacity**: 6 US quarts (minimum safe: 4 US quarts).
- **Electrical System**: 28V DC with 60A alternator and 24V battery.
