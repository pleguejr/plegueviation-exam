3. 
Pre-Flight Inspection 
Before each flight, it is necessary to carry out a complete aircraft check including a 
cabin inspection followed by an external inspection, as below detailed.  
 
For aircraft embodying MOD2010/267, the aircraft battery 
have the ability to be operational at temperatures down to -
40°C (-40°F) utilizing the internal, self-powered heater. 
Batteries pre-heat time will vary depending on temperature 
but can be fully warmed in 12 minutes or less when OAT is -
40°C (-40°F). Refer to battery manufacturer installation 
manual for additional information.

3.2. Aircraft Walk-Around 
To perform the aircraft walk around, carry out the checklists according to the pattern 
shown in  
Visual inspection is defined as follows: check for defects, 
cracks, detachments, excessive play, unsafe or improper 
installation as well as for general condition. For surfaces, visual 
inspection also involves additional check for freedom of 
movement and security. Red lubber lines on bolts and nuts shall 
be intact. 
Fuel level indicated by the fuel quantity indicators must be 
verified by visual check of actual fuel quantity in the tanks: 
graduated dipstick must be used. 
Fuel drainage operation must be carried out with the aircraft 
parked on a level surface. Set Cockpit Fuel Selector to LEFT or 
RIGHT prior to drain fuel.

Check to ensure no person or object is present in the area 
close to the propeller. Forward lower sector visibility is not 
possible from inside the cockpit. 
 
For operation with diesel, the aircraft must not be started if 
the temperature of the fuel in the tank is below -5°C (-10°C if 
use additive allowed by manufacturer’s specifications). 
If the grade of the fuel in the tank is unknown, always assume 
it is additive-free Diesel. 
 
Do not overheat the starter. Do not operate the starter for 
more than 10 seconds. After operating the starter, let it cool 
down for 20 seconds. After six attempts to start the engine, 
let the starter cool down for half an hour.

4.3. Warm Up 
1. 
Allow the engine to warm up for approximately 2 minutes at idle 
speed. 
For airfield elevation below 5500 ft: 
2. 
Increase RPM to 1400 until the Oil Temperature 50°C (122°F), 
Coolant Temperature 60°C (140°F). 
3. 
Continue according to Before taxing procedure. 
For airfield elevation at or above 5500 ft: 
2. 
Maintain Idle setting until Oil Temperature reaches 50°C (122°F) and 
Coolant Temperature reaches 60°C (140°F). 
3. 
Perform FADEC Test (point 7 of §4.6)  
4. 
Continue according to Before taxing procedure. 
 
Due to the increase of the idle speed with increasing pressure 
altitudes, the FADEC test is only possible to a limited extent 
from an airfield elevation of approximately 5500ft. 
Over 5500ft, the FADEC test is only possible if the load 
selector lever remains in the idle position after engine start 
until the FADEC test is starting. 
If the load selector lever is moved from the idle position, a 
FADEC test is no longer possible at pressure altitudes above 
5500ft. 
For this purpose, the engine has to be stopped and re-started 
to perform the FADEC test.

FADEC Test Button……………………... 
PRESS and HOLD button 
for entire test 
During the test, verify the following: 
1.
FADEC TEST CAS message comes ON and propeller speed
increases. Propeller speed target 1100-1300 RPM.
If the CAS messages do not appear at this time, a take-off must not 
be attempted with the aircraft. 
2.
The system then automatically switches to the FADEC B (FADEC-
B ACTIVE comes ON).
3.
The propeller governor is activated; the propeller speed decreases.
Propeller speed target 800-900 RPM.
4.
The system automatically switches to the FADEC A (FADEC-B
ACTIVE CAS message disappears), the propeller speed increases.
Propeller speed target 1100-1300 RPM.
5.
The propeller governor is activated; the propeller speed decreases.
Propeller speed target 800-900 RPM.
6.
FADEC TEST CAS message extinguishes, idle speed is reached;
the test is completed.
7.
Release test button.
If there are prolonged engine misfires or the engine shuts down
during the test, take off may not be attempted.
The whole FADEC-Test procedure has to be performed without any
failure. In case the engine shuts down or a FADEC warning is
indicated, take-off is prohibited. This applies even if the engine seems
to run without failure after the test.
