Cold Weather Operation* 
 P2010tdi is equipped with a sensor monitoring intercooler temperature. In case of both 
intercooler temperature equal or lower than 15°C (59°F) and altitude/Outside Air 
Temperature envelope within red area, a CAS message (“INTRCLR TEMP LO”) alerts 
the pilot. In this case, leave immediately this condition and manage the engine to restore 
allowed conditions (Refer to Par. 7.8.1 in Section 3).engine to restore allowed conditions 
(Refer to Par. 7.8.1 in Section 3). 
If continuous monitoring of intercooler temperature in-flight is not 
possible, engine operation in the red area should be avoided, due 
to the risk of an engine flame out. 
*) applicable for aircraft NOT embodying MOD 2010/240 - CD-170 upgrade for MAT 
limitation removal (Engine P/N 05-7200-K040301).

OPERATIVE AND ADDITIONAL LIMITATIONS 
EASA Approved 
P2010tdi – Aircraft Flight Manual Page 2 - 14
8.
Operative and additional limitations
8.1. Maximum Operating Altitude 
Maximum operating altitude is 18000 ft (5846 m) MSL 
Flight crew and passengers are required to use supplemental 
oxygen according to applicable Air Operation Rules. 
8.2. Flight Crew 
The minimum crew is one pilot seated in the left hand seat. 
8.3. Smoking 
Smoking is not allowed. 
8.4. Types of surface 
The take-off and landing can be conducted on hard paved or grass surfaces. 
8.5. Paint 
To ensure that the temperature of the composite structure does not exceed limits, the 
outer surface of the aeroplane must be painted with white paint, except for areas of 
registration marks, placards, and ornament. 
Refer to Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) for specific paint requirements.
